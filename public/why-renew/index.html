Preserve What You Already Own

Why Renew Your Roof?

An aging roof doesn't always mean it's time for a complete replacement. If your asphalt shingles are still in serviceable condition, rejuvenation may help preserve your roof and extend its useful life.

Understanding Roof Aging

Your Shingles Change As They Age

Asphalt shingles spend years exposed to sunlight, heat, rain, wind, and changing weather conditions. Over time, aging can cause shingles to become drier, less flexible, and more brittle.

You may begin noticing signs such as increased granule loss, curling, brittleness, fading, or shingles that simply don't feel as flexible as they once did.

That doesn't automatically mean the entire roof needs to be torn off and replaced.

?

Replace or Renew?

The important question isn't simply how old your roof is. It's whether the roof is still in suitable condition to benefit from preservation.

That's why every Shingle Renew Pro project starts with a roof assessment.

The Benefits

Reasons to Consider Roof Rejuvenation

01

Extend Useful Roof Life

Rejuvenation is designed to help preserve qualifying aging asphalt shingles so you can get more useful service from the roof you already own.

02

Restore Flexibility

As asphalt shingles age, they can become increasingly brittle. Rejuvenation is intended to help restore properties lost during the aging process and improve shingle flexibility.

03

Delay Major Expense

A complete roof replacement can be a significant investment. If your existing roof qualifies for rejuvenation, preservation may allow you to postpone that major expense.

04

Protect Your Investment

Your existing roof represents a substantial investment. Preserving serviceable shingles can make more sense than replacing them before their useful life is exhausted.

05

Reduce Roofing Waste

Extending the service life of an existing roof can help delay the removal and disposal of thousands of pounds of roofing material.

06

Less Disruption

Rejuvenating a qualifying roof avoids the extensive tear-off, material delivery, debris, noise, and disruption associated with a complete roof replacement.

Timing Matters

Don't Wait Until the Roof Is Too Far Gone

Roof rejuvenation is a preservation service — not a way to repair a roof that has already failed.

Severely damaged shingles, significant leaks, structural problems, or other advanced deterioration may mean replacement is the more appropriate option.

The best time to evaluate rejuvenation is while your roof still has useful life left to preserve.
