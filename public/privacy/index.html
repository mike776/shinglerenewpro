Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

Shingle Renew Pro respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly and transparently.

Last updated: August 14, 2026


Your privacy is important to us. It is Shingle Renew Pro's policy to respect your privacy and comply with applicable laws and regulations regarding personal information we may collect about you, including through https://ShingleRenewPro.com and other sites we own and operate.

Personal information is information that may be used to identify you. This may include information such as your name, address, phone number, email address, device information, payment information, or information about how you use a website or online service.

If our website contains links to third-party websites or services, please be aware that those websites may have their own privacy policies. This Privacy Policy does not apply to your activities after you leave our website.

Information We Collect

Information we collect generally falls into two categories: voluntarily provided information and automatically collected information.

You may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, including for SMS communication
  • Property or service location
  • Information about your roof or requested service
  • Other information relevant to your interaction with our services

Automatically collected information may include limited technical information sent by your device when accessing our website or services.

How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve our services
  • Respond to inquiries or service requests
  • Provide estimates or information about our services
  • Schedule inspections, appointments, or services
  • Communicate with you regarding your property or project
  • Send SMS messages when you have explicitly opted in to receive them
  • Maintain appropriate business records
  • Comply with legal obligations or enforce our terms and policies

We do not use your phone number or SMS opt-in information for marketing purposes unless you have provided explicit consent.

Shingle Renew Pro does not sell or rent your personal information. SMS opt-in information and phone numbers collected for SMS purposes are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Website and Technical Information

Like most websites, our website and hosting providers may automatically collect limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and basic website usage information.

This information may be used for website security, performance, troubleshooting, and general analytics.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for website functionality, security, preferences, or basic analytics.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features.

How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share limited information with trusted service providers when necessary to operate our website, communicate with customers, process service requests, or conduct normal business operations.

SMS opt-in information and phone numbers collected for SMS purposes are not shared with third parties or affiliate companies for marketing purposes.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to enforce our policies, or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, property, customers, or others.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, theft, unauthorized access, disclosure, copying, misuse, or modification.

However, no website, email system, electronic transmission method, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, normal business operations, recordkeeping, legal obligations, accounting requirements, or reporting requirements.

If personal information is no longer required for these purposes, we may delete it or make it anonymous by removing information that identifies you.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for homeowners and adults seeking information about roof rejuvenation and related services.

We do not direct our products or services to children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Rights and Choices

You do not have to provide personal information to us. However, choosing not to provide certain information may affect our ability to respond to your inquiry or provide requested services.

You may contact us to request access to personal information we hold about you or to ask that inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or misleading information be corrected.

Where permitted by applicable law, you may also request deletion of certain personal information we have collected.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable rights regarding your personal information.

Marketing and Communication Preferences

If you have previously agreed to receive marketing communications, you may change your mind at any time.

To unsubscribe from email communications, use the unsubscribe option provided in the message or contact us.

To opt out of SMS communications, reply STOP. For assistance, reply HELP. Message and data rates may apply and message frequency may vary.

Information Received From Third Parties

If we receive personal information about you from a third party, we will protect it in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

If you provide personal information about another person to us, you represent that you have permission to provide that information.

Business Transfers

If Shingle Renew Pro or its assets are acquired, sold, reorganized, or transferred, personal information may be included among the assets transferred as part of that transaction.

Any such transfer will be handled in accordance with applicable law and this Privacy Policy where required.

Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Shingle Renew Pro does not control those websites and is not responsible for their privacy practices, content, or security.

Data Breaches and Complaints

We will comply with applicable laws regarding notification of data breaches.

If you believe that we have improperly handled your personal information, please contact us and provide details of your concern. We will review reasonable complaints and respond as appropriate.

Additional U.S. State Privacy Rights

Residents of certain U.S. states, including California, Colorado, Delaware, Florida, Virginia, and Utah, may have additional privacy rights under applicable state law.

Depending on applicable law, these rights may include the ability to request:

  • The categories of personal information we have collected about you
  • The sources from which personal information was collected
  • The purposes for collecting or using personal information
  • The categories of personal information disclosed to third parties
  • The categories of third parties to whom information was disclosed
  • Specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you
  • Correction or deletion of certain personal information

To exercise applicable privacy rights, please contact us through the contact information provided on this website.

California Privacy Rights

California residents may have additional rights regarding personal information under applicable California privacy laws.

Where applicable, California residents may request information regarding certain disclosures of personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing purposes.

Requests may be submitted by contacting us and including “Privacy Rights Request” in your message.

Do Not Track

Some web browsers provide a “Do Not Track” setting that allows users to indicate that they do not want their online activities tracked. At this time, our website does not respond to browser Do Not Track signals.

Accessibility of This Policy

We are committed to transparency. A link to this Privacy Policy is available in the footer of our website.

Links to this Privacy Policy may also be provided near consent language or opt-in forms that collect personal information, including phone numbers used for SMS communications.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, business practices, legal requirements, or regulatory requirements.

The most recent version will be posted on this page with the updated date.

If required by law, we will obtain your permission or provide an opportunity to opt in or opt out of new uses of personal information.

Questions?

Contact Shingle Renew Pro

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to exercise applicable privacy rights, or have questions about how we handle personal information, please contact us through our website.
