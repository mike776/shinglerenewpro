Straight Answers

Questions homeowners actually ask

What is roof rejuvenation?

Roof rejuvenation is a preservation treatment for aging asphalt shingles.

As shingles age, the asphalt components can become dry and less flexible. A rejuvenation treatment is designed to penetrate the shingles and help restore properties that are lost through aging and weather exposure.

How do I know if my roof may be a good candidate?

Common signs of an aging asphalt shingle roof include granules collecting in gutters, shingles becoming dry or brittle, fading, weathering, minor cracking, and loss of flexibility.

Age alone does not determine whether a roof qualifies. The overall condition of the shingles, roof system, flashing, decking, and other components should be considered before treatment.

What happens if my roof does not qualify?

We will tell you. Roof rejuvenation is not appropriate for every roof.

If we find that the roof is too deteriorated, has significant damage, or needs repairs or replacement instead, we will explain what we found rather than recommending a treatment that is not appropriate for the roof.

How much longer can it extend the life of my roof?

The manufacturer of our product states that the treatment can add approximately six years of roof life. This should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every treated roof will last exactly six additional years.

Remaining roof life depends on many factors, including the roof’s current condition, installation quality, weather exposure, ventilation, previous damage, and maintenance.

Is rejuvenation less expensive than replacement?

For a roof that still qualifies for treatment, rejuvenation can be substantially less expensive than tearing off and replacing the entire roof.

The actual cost depends on factors such as roof size, pitch, accessibility, condition, preparation requirements, and repairs that may be needed. We provide pricing after evaluating the roof.

How long does the process take?

The amount of time required depends on the size, pitch, accessibility, cleanliness, and condition of the roof.

Preparation and treatment requirements can vary from one property to another, so we evaluate the roof before providing a project schedule.
