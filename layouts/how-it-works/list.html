Our Process

How Roof Rejuvenation Works

Your asphalt shingles don't necessarily need to be replaced just because they're getting older. Our process starts by determining whether your existing roof can be preserved.

A Simple Process

From Aging Shingles to a Renewed Roof

Asphalt shingles are exposed to years of Florida sun, heat, rain, wind, and changing weather conditions. As shingles age, they can gradually lose flexibility and become more brittle.

Shingle Renew Pro uses a professional roof rejuvenation process designed to help preserve qualifying asphalt shingle roofs and extend their useful service life.

01

Step One

Free Roof Assessment

Every roof is different. Before recommending rejuvenation, we inspect the condition of your asphalt shingles to determine whether the roof is a good candidate for treatment.

We look for the overall condition of the shingles, signs of excessive deterioration, damage, granule loss, and other conditions that could affect whether rejuvenation makes sense.

02

Step Two

Roof Preparation

If your roof qualifies, we prepare the roof and surrounding property for treatment.

Depending on the condition of the roof, preparation may include cleaning and removing surface contamination so the shingles are properly prepared for rejuvenation.

03

Step Three

Professional Application

Our Product is professionally applied across the asphalt shingle surface to provide consistent coverage throughout the qualifying areas of the roof.

This is not roof paint or a surface coating. The treatment is designed to interact with aging asphalt shingles rather than simply covering them.

04

Step Four

Absorption & Rejuvenation

After application, the rejuvenation treatment penetrates into the asphalt shingles.

The objective is to help restore properties lost through aging and improve the flexibility and condition of the existing shingles.

05

Step Five

Keep Your Roof Longer

Instead of replacing a roof that still has useful service life remaining, rejuvenation can help you preserve your existing roofing investment.

The result is a practical roof preservation option for homeowners and property owners who want to get more useful life from a qualifying asphalt shingle roof.

Important Difference

Rejuvenation Is Not a Roof Coating

We aren't painting your shingles or covering the roof with a waterproof coating. Roof rejuvenation is a preservation treatment intended for qualifying aging asphalt shingles.
