Why We Started Shingle Renew Pro

Before starting Shingle Renew Pro, we spent considerable time researching the roof rejuvenation industry — how different companies approach the process, the products and methods being used, and what actually matters when treating aging asphalt shingles.

An aging roof doesn't always mean it's time for a new roof.

As asphalt shingles age, they can lose flexibility and weather resistance even though the roof may still have years of useful service left.

Roof rejuvenation offers homeowners another option — one designed to help extend the useful life of qualifying asphalt-shingle roofs and delay premature replacement.

A Practical Alternative to Roof Replacement

Our goal isn't to convince every homeowner that their roof needs treatment. Some roofs genuinely need replacement, and rejuvenation isn't appropriate for every roof.

Our goal is to evaluate the roof honestly and, when it's a good candidate, provide an affordable option that can help homeowners protect their existing roof, extend its useful life, and postpone the significant expense of replacement.

Quality Without the Big-Company Price

We're intentionally keeping Shingle Renew Pro a lean, locally operated business.

Without the large overhead and layers of expenses associated with many bigger companies, we're able to keep our pricing competitive while focusing our resources where they matter most: quality products, proper application, dependable service, and our customers' roofs.

We believe homeowners shouldn't have to choose between quality and affordability.
