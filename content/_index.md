## Is Your Roof Showing Signs of Aging?

You may be a candidate for roof rejuvenation if you're seeing:

- Granules accumulating in your gutters
- Shingles becoming dry or brittle
- Fading or weathered appearance
- Minor cracking or loss of flexibility
- An asphalt shingle roof that's approaching or beyond 10 years old
