---
title: "Is roof rejuvenation the same as roof cleaning?"
weight: 40
---

No.

Roof cleaning is primarily intended to remove algae, organic growth, dirt, staining, and other surface contamination.

Roof rejuvenation is intended to help preserve the physical properties of aging asphalt shingles.

Depending on the condition of the roof, cleaning or other preparation may be recommended before rejuvenation.
