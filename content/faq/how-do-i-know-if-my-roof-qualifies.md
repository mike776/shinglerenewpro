---
title: "How do I know if my roof may be a good candidate?"
weight: 60
---

Some common signs of an aging asphalt shingle roof include granules collecting in gutters, shingles becoming dry or brittle, fading, weathering, minor cracking, and loss of flexibility.

Age alone does not determine whether a roof qualifies.

The overall condition of the shingles, roof system, flashing, decking, and other components should be considered before treatment.
