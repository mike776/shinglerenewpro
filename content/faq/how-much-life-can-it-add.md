---
title: "How much longer can roof rejuvenation extend the life of my roof?"
weight: 90
---

The manufacturer of Our Product states that the treatment can add approximately six years of roof life.

This should not be interpreted as a guarantee that every treated roof will last exactly six additional years.

Remaining roof life depends on many factors, including the roof's current condition, installation quality, weather exposure, ventilation, previous damage, and maintenance.
