---
title: "How does Shingle Renew Pro work?"
weight: 20
---

We begin by evaluating the condition of your asphalt shingle roof to determine whether rejuvenation is appropriate.

If the roof is a good candidate, it is prepared for treatment and the rejuvenation product is applied evenly across the shingle surface.

The goal is to preserve a roof that still has useful service life remaining rather than replacing it prematurely.
