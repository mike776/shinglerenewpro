---
title: "Will rejuvenation change the color of my roof?"
weight: 120
---

The primary purpose of roof rejuvenation is preservation, not changing the color of the roof.

The appearance of weathered shingles may improve after treatment and preparation, but results will vary depending on shingle age, original color, staining, algae growth, and granule condition.

It should not be considered a substitute for painting or installing a different shingle color.
