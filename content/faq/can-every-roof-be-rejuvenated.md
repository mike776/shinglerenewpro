---
title: "Can every asphalt shingle roof be rejuvenated?"
weight: 50
---

No.

Roof rejuvenation is intended for aging asphalt shingle roofs that are still structurally serviceable.

A roof with extensive leaks, severely deteriorated shingles, major storm damage, rotten decking, widespread missing shingles, or other serious problems may require repairs or replacement instead.

That is why we evaluate the roof before recommending treatment.
